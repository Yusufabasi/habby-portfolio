Community-Based Care

Obstetric Fistula Community-Based Assessment Tool (OF-COMBAT)

International Journal for Recent Research in Social Sciences and Humanity

Presents the development and piloting of the OF-COMBAT — the first-ever verbal screening tool for obstetric fistula, achieving over 85% screening success. Examines gaps in post-repair follow-up and proposes community-based models to improve long-term outcomes for fistula survivors. A landmark contribution to frontline fistula identification and referral.

Psychosocial Health

Psychosocial Consequences of Obstetric Fistula in Western Kenya

Research Publication

Examines the psychological and social burden of obstetric fistula on survivors in western Kenya, drawing on direct counselling experience and field data. Explores the intersection of mental health, social stigma, and recovery — and makes the case for integrating psychosocial support into standard fistula care pathways.

Original Contributions

Tools & Frameworks Developed

Instruments, manuals, and models created or co-developed — now used by governments, NGOs, and health providers across Africa.

Screening Tool

OF-COMBAT

The Obstetric Fistula Community-Based Assessment Tool — the first-ever verbal screening instrument for fistula, achieving 85%+ success. Developed and piloted across Kenya; adopted as a model for community-level identification.

Clinical Manual

The Orange Manual

Contributor to the development and publishing of the Orange Manual — a definitive clinical reference on obstetric and other forms of female genital fistula, guiding principles for clinical care and management across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Training Manual

Female Genital Fistula CHV Training Manual

Lead consultant in developing and piloting the Female Genital Fistula training manual for Community Health Volunteers, in collaboration with the Ministry of Health — the first structured CHV fistula training programme in Kenya.

Assessment Tools

RMNCAH Training & Assessment Tools

Led the design and rollout of RMNCAH training and assessment tools for community health volunteers and frontline providers, improving early identification and referral of high-risk pregnancies and childbirth injuries.

Psychosocial Intervention

PM+ Pilot — Fistula Survivors

Successfully piloted the Problem Management Plus (PM+) psychological intervention with fistula survivors across three counties in Kenya — a WHO-developed low-intensity intervention adapted for post-repair psychosocial recovery.

Strategic Framework

Kenya Female Genital Fistula Strategic Framework

Technical contributor to the development of Kenya's national Female Genital Fistula strategic framework — shaping the policy and programmatic landscape for fistula care at a national level.

Building care systems that are rooted in community realities — survivor-led, culturally sensitive, and economically sustainable. From solidarity groups to microenterprise programmes, the community is the system.
