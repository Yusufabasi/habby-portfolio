About

Building Systems That Last

I am a psychologist and program specialist with over two decades of experience designing and leading complex, multi-country health programs across Sub-Saharan Africa.

My work focuses on building integrated systems that connect clinical care, mental health, and community-based support to create sustainable recovery pathways for women and girls.

I am the Founder and Lead Director of WADADIA, where we advance holistic care through the HER Dignity model — combining clinical treatment, psychosocial support, and socio-economic empowerment.

Previously, I served as Regional Director of Programs in Africa, overseeing initiatives across 29 countries in partnership with governments and global institutions.
